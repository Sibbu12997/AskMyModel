# Getting support

Open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/Sibbu12997/AskMyModel/issues and include:

- What you expected and what happened.
- Steps to reproduce and the exact sanitized error.
- Power BI Desktop, Node.js, PowerShell, Ollama, and AskMyModel versions.
- Whether `/health` responds and whether the metadata exporter completed.

Do not upload PBIX files, business data, credentials, or unredacted metadata/logs. AskMyModel is currently intended for Power BI Desktop on Windows; Power BI Service deployment is not included.
